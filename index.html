AI tools worth your time

No hype, no jargon. Just what's actually useful for teachers, tested before it's recommended.

Claude
Best for: creating games, guiding planning

Let's get building! Claude builds games with ease, helping engage students in personalised learning. Build projects, feeding every piece of information needed to make Claude the best sidekick possible.

Copilot
Best for: creating agents, images and your day-to-day assistant.

If your school runs on Word, Outlook and Teams, Copilot meets you there. Drafting newsletters and summarising meetings. Create agents to work with you and save you time. Even small tasks can be compelted by an agent.

Gemini
Best for: fast ideas, Google Workspace, image creation.

Quick brainstorming, planning ideas, and slide or image generation. Built into Docs, Slides and Classroom for schools already living in Google. Amazing image creation.
